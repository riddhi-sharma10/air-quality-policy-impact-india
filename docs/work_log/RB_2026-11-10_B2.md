4. Rules frozen before any post-event result is seen
   - **Events:** primary = the 2 primary episodes from B1 (11 Nov 2025, 13 Dec 2025); relaxed adds 19 Oct 2025. Day 0 = IST date of the first change; the "last day" L = date of the last change of the episode. Day 0 and the transition day(s) are not used.
   - **Outcome:** log of daily mean PM2.5. **Effect windows:** days 1 to 3 after L (primary), days 4 to 7 (secondary).
   - **Model (fixed):** log(PM2.5) on log boundary layer height, wind speed, 2 m temperature, relative humidity, a rain flag (daily rain above 0.1 mm) and log(1 + upwind fire count, previous day). No lagged PM2.5 (it would carry post-event values into the prediction). No stage variable.
   - **Fit window:** the 60 days before day 0 (days -60 to -1), dropping invalid PM2.5 days, days with missing fire values, and the 3 days after any other stage change.
   - **Counterfactual quality rule:** leave-block-out cross-validation in the fit window (blocks of 3 consecutive valid days); CV R2 of at least 0.40, otherwise the event is flagged "weak counterfactual" and reported but not pooled.
   - **Interval:** effect (mean log difference over the window) plus or minus t(0.975) x the standard deviation of the block-mean cross-validation errors (block length equal to the window length). This includes parameter uncertainty and the day-to-day correlation within a block. Effects are shown as percent change (exp(d) - 1) and in µg/m³ (actual mean minus predicted mean with the smearing correction).
   - **Pooling:** inverse-variance weighted mean of the events (fixed effect), and always the individual events. With 2 or 3 events no between-event variance can be estimated; this is stated in every table and figure.
   - **Placebo events (mean-reversion check), treated stations:** dates with no upward stage change from 3 days before to 7 days after, a 7-day pre-mean of PM2.5 within 0.75 to 1.33 times the real event's pre-mean, valid fit and post days, same model and windows. Report the share of placebo effects at or below the real effect (one-sided). **Placebo, control cities:** the real event dates with the same method on the control series.
   - **Cross-check A:** OLS of log PM2.5 on the same controls plus a post indicator (days 1 to 7 after L) and a linear time term, on days -30 to +7, Newey-West (lag 3) standard errors.
   - **Status of H2 (plan 5.4):** supported only if the pooled day 1 to 3 effect is below 0 with a 95% interval excluding 0, **and** each primary event lies at or below the 10th percentile of its placebo distribution, **and** the sign agrees in at least 75% of the B3 checks. Not supported if the interval excludes 0 on the positive side or rules out a fall of 10% or more. Anything else: inconclusive.
5. Risks
